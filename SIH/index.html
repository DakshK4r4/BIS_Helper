IS 302-1

Active

Safety of Household and Similar Electrical Appliances – Part 1: General Requirements

Explain Simply (AI Summary)

This standard specifies safety rules for electrical appliances used at home and similar places. It ensures protection against electric shock, thermal hazards, mechanical injuries, and fire hazards.

Applicable Products:

    Household electrical appliances, kitchen equipment, heating devices.

Key Requirements

Protection Against Access to Live Parts

Appliances must be designed so that live parts cannot be touched accidentally.

Heating & Thermal Insulation

Surface temperatures must not exceed specified safety limits under normal operating conditions.
